Attack interval

Attack Interval represents the delay between attacks made by the unit, which is shown in seconds (e.g. Exusiai, who has an attack interval of 1, attacks once every second) and influenced by the unit's attack speed or ASPD, which will be further explained below.

An Operator's attack interval is not directly shown, instead displayed as one of the following five ratings which roughly determines their value:

Very Fast <0.8
Fast 0.8 ~ 1
Average 1 ~ 1.2
Slightly Slow 1.2 ~ 1.6
Slow >1.6

Prior to the Hortus de Escapismo update, enemies' attack intervals were not shown in-game. After the update, they are now displayed as one of the following ten ratings which roughly determines their value:

SS <0.5
S+ 0.5 – 0.8
S 0.8 – 1
A+ 1 – 1.2
A 1.2 – 1.7
B+ 1.7 – 2.6
B 2.6 – 3.5
C 3.5 – 5
D 5 – 6.9
E ≥7

Cycle[edit]

Attack interval includes three phases: Wind-up, Launch, and Wind-down.

Wind-up Phase[edit]

When a target is eligible to be chosen by the attacking unit, the unit will enter Wind-up Phase. The attacker will immediately choose a target according to Aggression. The attacking animation is played and the animation timer starts.

The length of the Wind-up Phase depends on the animation length. For example, Siege has a longer Wind-up Phase than Saga.

Launch Phase[edit]

When the Wind-up Phase ends, the attack is launched towards the target.

  • If there is no projectile, then the attack immediately hits the target and the damage of the attack is calculated.
  • If the attack launches a projectile, then the damage calculation occurs at the moment the projectile impacts the target. Buffs and debuffs can begin or cease to apply mid-flight.

If the attack is aborted before the Launch Phase, the attack is cancelled and the attack interval is cleared. If the attack is aborted during or after the Launch Phase, the attack is cancelled, but any skills attached to that attack (e.g. Power Strike α/β/γ or Bombtail's bomb attack) and on-attack talent effects (e.g. Platinum's ATK buff from All-Out Attack) do not reset and must recharge if possible. Infused Originium Slugs and other enemies with a similar on-death effect launch an invisible projectile toward their own position upon death with a "travel time" equal to their Wind-down animation duration.

For channeled skills (e.g. Angelina's second and third skills or Eyjafjalla's third skill), a channel animation plays on a loop, with attacks launched according to the attack interval for the duration of the skill.

Wind-down Phase[edit]

When the Launch phase is finished and any projectile has been launched, the Wind-down Phase starts and an animation is played.

If the Wind-down Phase ends and the animation timer is shorter than the attack interval, the attacker will return to the idle state before the next Wind-up Phase.

If the calculated attack interval is shorter than the combined duration of the Wind-up and Wind-down animations, the animations will be shown in a faster speed.

Modifiers[edit]

There are two ways a unit's attack interval is changed: direct attack interval modifiers and ASPD modifiers.

Direct[edit]

Direct modifiers increase or decrease the attack interval itself and are applied before ASPD modifiers. Direct interval modifiers only affect the unit themselves and there can ever only be one direct interval modifier (whether increase or decrease) active on a single unit at a time.

Direct interval modifiers can be distinguished through the mention of "Attack Interval" in the talent/skill/etc. description, though not all are stated in-game, and can be fixed (Pozëmka's Opprobrium), multiplicative (Harmonie's Lithe Steps), or percentage-based (Ash's Assault Tactics).

ASPD[edit]

ASPD modifiers increase/decrease the attack interval by a set percentage of the attack interval (though listed as a decimal number in-game), with flat modifiers stacking additively, and percentage modifiers stacking multiplicatively?. For example, "ASPD +5" and "ASPD +10" together are the same as "ASPD +15", indicating a 15%? increase in "attack speed" and therefore reducing the attack interval by 13.0%?.

ASPD modifiers can be distinguished through the mention of "ASPD" in the talent/skill/etc. description. All units have 100 ASPD by default, and the final ASPD value can never go below 20 or above 600.

Attack Interval calculation[edit]

A unit's Attack Interval is calculated using the following formulas:

AtkTime=(AtkTimebase+AtkTimeflat)*(1+AtkTimepercent)*AtkTimemultiplicative?

Where:

  • AtkTime represents the Attack Interval after applying all direct Attack Interval modifiers in seconds.
  • AtkTimebase represents the base Attack Interval of a unit in seconds.
  • AtkTimeflat represents the flat Attack Interval modifier in seconds, if any.
  • AtkTimepercent represents the percentage-based Attack Interval modifier, if any.
  • AtkTimemultiplicative represents the multiplicative Attack Interval modifier, if any.

AtkSpeed=100(100+ASPDflat)*∏(1+ASPDpercent)

Where:

  • AtkSpeed represents the relative increase/decrease from all ASPD modifiers.
  • ASPDflat represents the sum of all flat ASPD modifiers.
  • ASPDpercent represents any and all percentage-based ASPD modifiers.
  • ∏, also known as product, represents the action of multiplying all values together.

AtkInterval=AtkTime*AtkSpeed

Where:

  • AtkInterval represents the final Attack Interval.

Buffs[edit]

Friendly[edit]

Many Operators have the ability to increase the ASPD of friendly units (including themselves) and reduce their own attack interval, but those will not be listed in this page due to their numbers.

One of the most notable examples are the Tactical Chant and Swift Strike skills, which increases the user's ASPD for their duration.

  • Dominions created by Emperor's Blade, "Pursuer" affect enemies as well once the Pursuer is defeated.
  • In Expeditioner's Joklumarkar, the Propagating Collapse Collapsal Paradigm increases the Dominions' ASPD debuff to 75.
